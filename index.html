Gridfall

3D turn-based strategy · four armies

Command cyan forces from the southwest corner. Three rival armies hold the other corners. Win by wiping out rival armies. High ground and clustered units hit harder. Enemy armies scout in fog until they make contact. Each army fields 10 units.

How to Play

  1. Select one of your cyan units by tapping it.
  2. Move by tapping a blue-highlighted tile within range.
  3. Attack by tapping a red-highlighted enemy in range (after or instead of moving, once per unit).
  4. Tap End Turn — Ember, Ash, and Cinder move automatically.
  5. Win by defeating all rival units (Ember, Ash, and Cinder). Lose if all of your units fall.
  6. Terrain — rolling high/low ground (neighbor height shifts stay within 2%). Attacking from higher ground deals +2% damage.
  7. Formation — adjacent allies (including diagonals) strengthen attacks: +4% when paired, +8% when 3+ are together.
  8. Facing — strike any of the 8 neighboring tiles (front, sides, back, diagonals).
  9. Archers — shoot adjacent by default; range extends up to 3 through empty tiles or over friendlies (not through enemies/rocks).
  10. Fog of war (AI) — rival armies do not know your positions until a unit is within 1 tile; they scout separately and will not all pile on at once from the start.
Infantry — Move 3, melee attack 1 tile, balanced.
Archer — Move 2, attack up to 3 tiles (cannot shoot adjacent).
Bastion — Move 2, tough armor, melee smash.
Rival armies — Ember (orange), Ash (purple), Cinder (rose) — 10 units each (1 Bastion, 5 Infantry, 4 Archers), one per corner.

Victory

You cleared the Ember forces.
